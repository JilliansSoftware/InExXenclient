== Silver wood of the Papuan wilds | 18 Nov
- Gnarled cypresses grow in the jungle west of Papua.
- They are now the only source of silver wood for lumberjacks.

== Polished window lettering | 18 Nov
- Gilded letters in the journal, skills and paperdoll; dark text is engraved.
- Health bars: painted fills, carved sockets and names on a ribbon.
- The journal has a title and room to breathe; the skills window shows your total on a plate.
- Resize corners are easier to grab.

== Smoother zoom | 17 Nov
- Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms the game view, and it glides instead of stepping.
